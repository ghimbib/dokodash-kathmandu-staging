# Monsoon Trail Asset Notes

Runtime art for the one-level `?prototype=monsoon-trail` staging sample.

## Generated Art

Created for DokoDash with OpenAI image generation on 2026-07-30:

- `monsoon-trail-key-art.webp`: character-select and loading key art.
- `monsoon-trail-world.webp`: clean Bandipur-inspired mid-hill environment plate.
- `sluice-gate.webp`: handmade fieldstone and sal-wood irrigation sluice.
- `rice-paddy.webp`: young rice plants in a shallow monsoon paddy.
- `rice-seedlings.webp`: realistic-scale young rice vegetation clusters.
- `mud-cut.webp`: irregular puddle integrated into a wet stone footpath.
- `doko-food-cache.webp`: woven doko with Nepali trail foods.
- `pipal-chautari.webp`: pipal-tree chautari and rain shelter.

The runtime files are compressed derivatives. Original generated PNGs remain in
the originating Codex task's generated-image workspace.

## Cultural Direction

- The setting is a fictional Bandipur-inspired mid-hill ridge after monsoon rain,
  not a literal reconstruction of one village.
- Rice terraces, field irrigation, local fieldstone, dark timber, slate roofs,
  woven doko baskets, and a pipal-tree chautari are treated as everyday landscape
  and travel infrastructure.
- Sacred architecture is environmental context only. It is never an enemy,
  destructible target, currency, or power-up.
- Food boosts use momo, sel roti, and dal bhat without presenting them as generic
  fantasy objects detached from the trail setting.
